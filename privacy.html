Privacy Policy

Tenths: Metrology & Inspection

Effective date: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy covers the Tenths: Metrology & Inspection app for Android (package com.tenthsapp.metrology, “the app”), published by Midwest Solutions LLC (“we”, “us”). The short version: the app does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

1. Information we collect

None. The app has no user accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking or advertising SDKs. We do not collect your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers or usage data.

2. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the app saves the following only in local storage on your own device: which lessons you have marked complete, your quiz scores and badge status, your simulator practice counts and best scores, and (only if you choose to type it) a display name for your skills record. This information is not sent to us and we cannot access it.

If you have Android's own device backup turned on, Android may include this app's saved progress in your personal Google backup so it can be restored on a new device. That backup is operated by Google under your Google account and is not accessible to us. You can turn it off in your phone's Settings under Backup.

3. Network use

All lessons, diagrams, simulators and reference data are bundled inside the app and work offline. The app does not send any data to us or to any third party.

4. Permissions

The app does not ask for access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos, files or advertising ID. It declares Android's standard internet permission because the built-in web component that displays its pages expects it, but the app does not use it to send or receive any data.

5. Deleting your data

You can erase everything the app has stored at any time by using Progress → Reset all progress, by clearing the app's storage in your device settings, or by uninstalling the app. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

6. Children's privacy

The app is an educational tool intended for a general audience of students, apprentices and working professionals. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Third parties

The app is distributed through Google Play. Google may collect information about installs, purchases and crashes under its own privacy policy, independently of the app. We do not receive personal data from Google about individual users.

8. Your rights

Privacy laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and CCPA/CPRA give people rights over personal data held about them. Since we do not collect or hold personal data through the app, there is no personal data for us to provide, correct, delete or sell. If you have questions, contact us and we will respond.

9. Changes to this policy

If a future version of the app changes how data is handled, we will update this policy and its effective date before that version is released.
